☁ Sync between your PCs

BudgetPress keeps this budget in one file inside a folder that your cloud drive already syncs – OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud Drive. Open the same file on your other PCs and they stay up to date. No BudgetPress account, no BudgetPress server.

With a password, the file is unreadable to anyone else – even if someone gets into your cloud drive. You need the same password on your other PCs. It cannot be recovered if you forget it.
