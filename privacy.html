BudgetPress – Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

BudgetPress does not collect, send or sell any personal or financial information. Your budgets, accounts and transactions are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. BudgetPress never connects to your bank; bank files you import are read on your device only.

BudgetPress has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Data leaves your device only when you save a backup or export file yourself, or when you turn on sync: then BudgetPress writes your budget to a file in a folder you choose (for example your OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox folder), encrypted if you set a password. That cloud service moves the file under its own privacy terms; BudgetPress never sends data to any server of its own.

The optional PIN is stored on your device as a one-way hash.

Purchases of BudgetPress Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store. BudgetPress only asks the Store whether Pro is owned; it never sees your payment details.

You can delete your data at any time with ☰ More → Start over, by deleting a budget, or by uninstalling the app.
