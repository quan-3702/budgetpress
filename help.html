💰 How to use BudgetPress

BudgetPress uses envelope budgeting: you only budget money you already have, and you give every dollar a job. Everything stays on this computer – no account, no bank login, no subscription.

Want to look around first? ☰ More → Load a sample budget. You can start again any time with ☰ More → Start over.

1. Add your accounts

In 🏦 Accounts, add your checking, savings and cash with today's balance. That money becomes Ready to Assign. Add credit cards with what you owe. Loans, a house or investments can be added as tracking accounts – they count toward net worth but not your budget.

2. Give every dollar a job

In 💰 Budget, type amounts in the Assigned column until Ready to Assign reaches zero. Available is what is left in each envelope; it rolls over to next month.

  • Green – money available. Yellow – a goal still needs money this month. Red – overspent: click it to move money from somewhere else.
  • ⚡ Auto-assign fills the month from your goals, last month, or your 3-month averages.
  • ⇄ Move money moves money between envelopes – or click any Available amount.
  • Click a category to rename it, move it, hide it, add a note or set a goal: every month, refill up to, every week, by a date, or every year (e.g. insurance).
  • Click a group name to add a category, rename or delete the group.

3. Record what you spend and earn

Click ＋ Transaction (or press N). Income goes to Ready to Assign. Spending goes to a category – the payee remembers its category next time.

  • Split one receipt across several categories.
  • Transfer between accounts – this is how you pay a credit card.
  • Flags in six colors, and a ✓ cleared mark for things that show on your bank statement.
  • Reconcile: filter to one account, click ✔ Reconcile and type your bank's balance.
  • Import CSV, OFX or QFX files downloaded from your bank (☰ More). Duplicates are skipped.
  • Undo anything with Ctrl+Z.

4. Credit cards

Each card gets an envelope in Credit Card Payments. When you buy something on the card from a budgeted category, the money automatically moves into the card's payment envelope – so it is ready when the bill comes. Pay the card with a Transfer from checking. For debt you already had, assign money to the payment envelope.

5. Reports and planning

  • 📊 Reports: spending by category, 12 months of income vs spending, net worth, and Age of Money (how many days your money waits before it is spent – aim for 30+).
  • 📅 Plan: a 60-day balance forecast from your scheduled bills and paychecks, a bill calendar, and a debt payoff plan (avalanche or snowball) showing your debt-free date and the interest you save.
  • 🖨 Cash envelopes (☰ More): print a labelled envelope with a spending log for every category you assigned money to – perfect for cash stuffing.

6. Keep it safe

Save a backup file now and then (☰ More). Lock BudgetPress with a PIN if others use your PC. Keep several budgets – personal, household, side business – with 📒 My budgets.

7. Use it on several PCs

☰ More → ☁ Sync between PCs. Click Create a sync file and save it inside your OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud Drive folder (add a password to encrypt it). On your other PC, install BudgetPress, open the same dialog, type the same password and choose Use a sync file from another PC. Changes go both ways a few seconds after you make them, when you switch back to the app, and every minute. If you change things on both PCs while offline, BudgetPress combines them. The ☁ button in the top bar shows when it last synced. Your cloud drive moves the file – there is no BudgetPress server or account.
